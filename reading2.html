Reading “With ‘AIi shop” distorting our reality the world is sleepwalking into disaster” by Nersine Malik made me think more seriously about how AI affects what we may see online. Before this article I knew AI could create images and write text but I did not realize how much low quality and misleading content it could produce so quickly. The term “ai slop” stood out to me because it describes content that is made just to grab attention, not inform people accurately. What really impacted me was the idea that society is “sleepwalking” into a problem. Many of us scroll through social media without questioning where content comes from. If ai-generated material looks real or professional we often accept it without thinking twice. This can slowly change how we understand news, politics, and important issues. Over time it becomes harder to separate truth from misinformation. It showed me that the danger is not just the technology itself but how easily society accepts and spreads its output without thinking critically. After reading this I feel more motivated to be careful about information I consume and share because the future of public understanding may depend on it.
